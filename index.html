About the Studio

A salon

Welcome to Hair By Sarah — a private luxury hair studio nestled in a beautifully converted home garage in Wardle, Rochdale. Here, you always get Sarah's complete attention in a calm, unhurried space designed entirely around you.

With years of expertise across colour, cutting and styling, Sarah crafts results that are as unique as you are.
